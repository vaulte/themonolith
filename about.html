Overview

The Central Authority of Safety (CAS) is a centralized administrative authority responsible for the development, implementation and maintenance of safety standards across designated areas of operation.

The Authority provides administrative, technical and operational support to government departments, public institutions and other authorized bodies where required.

CAS maintains a network of administrative offices, operational facilities and specialist departments to support its functions.

Organization

The Central Authority of Safety is organized into administrative and operational divisions according to current organizational requirements.

Divisions are responsible for the management of specific functions and may establish subordinate units where appropriate.

  • Central Administration
  • Facilities Management
  • Personnel Administration
  • Safety & Compliance
  • Research & Development
  • Special Operations

Organizational structures are reviewed periodically and may be amended without public notice.

Administration

CAS administrative functions are coordinated through the Central Administrative Facility.

Administrative responsibilities include records management, personnel coordination, procurement, facilities oversight, internal communications and regulatory compliance.

Requests concerning internal administrative matters should be directed to the appropriate departmental office.

Facilities

CAS operates and maintains facilities in accordance with applicable safety, security and operational requirements.

Facilities may differ in function, size and operational capacity depending on departmental requirements.

Information concerning facility locations, operating schedules and access requirements may be subject to authorization.

Facility information displayed through this website represents publicly releasable information only.

Personnel

CAS employs personnel across a range of administrative, technical, scientific and operational positions.

Personnel appointments are made according to organizational requirements and applicable employment standards.

Employees are required to comply with applicable workplace policies, confidentiality requirements, safety procedures and departmental regulations.

Standards & Procedures

CAS develops and maintains internal standards, procedures and operational guidelines relating to organizational activities.

Procedures are reviewed periodically to ensure continued suitability and compliance with current requirements.

Employees may access applicable procedures through the internal information management system.

Organizational History

The Central Authority of Safety was established in 1958 following administrative consolidation of several existing safety and regulatory functions.

Since its establishment, the Authority has undergone periodic organizational restructuring in response to changes in government policy, operational requirements and public safety considerations.

Additional historical information is maintained by the CAS Records and Archives Department.

Historical records may contain terminology, organizational classifications and references that are no longer in current use.

Public Information

The CAS public information portal provides access to general organizational information and publicly releasable records.

Information not intended for public distribution is maintained within the Authority's internal information management systems.

Requests for information may be submitted through the appropriate administrative channels.
