FACILITY OVERVIEW LCTN-S-001

The Central Administration Facility serves as the principal administrative headquarters of the Central Authority of Safety.

The facility accommodates senior administration, departmental offices, personnel services, records management and national operational coordination.

Facility operations are conducted during standard government working hours, with selected departments maintaining extended operational schedules.

OPERATING INFORMATION FAC-01.04

The Monolith, also known as Site-001, is a research, control, and containment site that is operated by the Central Authority of Safety. This is known as the central research hub for the Authority and operations centre. While attempts to map out the Monolith, no direct map of it can ever be given or created due to the ever shifting labyrinth that is the Monolith's internal layout as it defies all placed laws of reality and space. While the interior of the Monolith shifts on a daily basis, the exterior of the Monolith has remained as the Soviet Brutalist skyscraper since the the day of its discovery.

Located in the downtown central business district of Washington DC, and possesses a form of perception filter, allowing it to be bypassed by the normal eye unless you are already aware of the building or specifically looking for it. It often appears in the corner of the human eye but can be see by animals and often has stray animals wandering in and out. The Monolith cannot have photographs, videos or any other other form of capture taken of it unless the object capturing the image is itself an anomalous object.
