FACILITY DESCRIPTION

Facility 01 serves as the central administrative headquarters of the Directorate.

The facility houses administrative offices, departmental coordination services, public information services, personnel management, records administration, and executive offices.

The facility is accessible to authorised Directorate personnel and members of the public during designated operating hours.
