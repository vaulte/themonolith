FACILITY OVERVIEW FAC-01.01

The Central Administration Facility serves as the principal administrative headquarters of the Central Authority of Safety.

The facility accommodates senior administration, departmental offices, personnel services, records management and national operational coordination.

Facility operations are conducted during standard government working hours, with selected departments maintaining extended operational schedules.

OPERATING INFORMATION FAC-01.04

Normal facility operating hours are Monday through Friday, 08:00–17:00.

Personnel with approved after-hours access may enter the facility outside standard operating hours.

All visitors must report to the main reception desk upon arrival and present valid identification.
