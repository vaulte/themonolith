FACILITY DESCRIPTION

Facility 04 is designated as a biological research and environmental monitoring facility operated by the Directorate.

The facility conducts environmental sampling, biological observation and controlled research activities.

[CONTENT REMOVED]

All research conducted at Facility 04 is subject to Directorate Research Regulation 7.14.

Personnel are reminded that environmental observations must be recorded from the designated observation areas.
