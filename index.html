National Research & Security Administration

Welcome

The Directorate is a government research and administrative organisation responsible for the investigation, monitoring and management of matters considered to be of national significance.

This portal provides access to publicly available information concerning Directorate facilities, research programmes, personnel and historical records.

Statement of Purpose

The Directorate exists to ensure that matters requiring specialised investigation, scientific expertise or extraordinary administrative oversight are addressed in accordance with national policy.

Personnel are reminded that information obtained through Directorate activities may be subject to statutory confidentiality requirements.
