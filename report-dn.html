GENERAL DESCRIPTION DESC-017

CANLY-A-789 is a hardcover detective novel of unknown origin. The object possesses no visible title, author, publisher or publication markings. The only identifying information present on the exterior of the object is an ISBN printed in small white characters on the lower portion of the rear cover.

The ISBN is structurally valid but does not correspond to any known publication. Searches conducted across national and international bibliographic databases have returned no result.

The contents of the object appear to constitute a detective noir novel. This description is intentionally vague.

The plot, characters, setting, chronology and prose style have changed between every recorded reading.

The object itself does not otherwise change.

RESEARCHER NOTES NOTES / DR. ███████
03.04.2021 / INITIAL EXAMINATION

It is a detective novel. I don't know what else you want me to say. There is a detective. There is a murder. There is a woman in a red dress. At least there was yesterday.

04.04.2021 / SECOND READING

The woman in the red dress is now the murderer. She wasn't yesterday. The detective is also apparently dead. I checked. He was alive yesterday.

05.04.2021 / PHOTOGRAPHIC TEST

Camera produced a photograph of the table on which the book was sitting. The book itself was absent from the image. It was physically present. I am aware of how that sounds.

07.04.2021 / SCANNING TEST

Scanner produced 312 blank pages. The scanner was functioning normally. The book was not blank.

09.04.2021 / THIRD READING

The detective is now called Arthur again. This is not the same Arthur. I am almost certain this is not the same Arthur. The murder is also different. Why does it keep doing this?

10.04.2021 / ADMINISTRATIVE NOTE

I have been instructed to "continue documenting narrative variation." I have documented narrative variation. There is narrative variation. The narrative varies. I don't know what else to write.

11.04.2021 / FINAL NOTE

I am not reading the book again. Assign someone else.
