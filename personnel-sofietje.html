Background & Employment History

Eleanor Margaret Voss was born in Northbridge in 1968 and was raised by two government-employed parents. Her father, Martin Voss, worked within the Department of Infrastructure Planning. Her mother, Helen Voss, was employed by the National Environmental Survey.

Voss entered government service immediately following completion of her undergraduate studies. Her initial appointment with the Central Authority of Safety was recorded in September 1987 as Junior Environmental Assessment Officer.

Between 1989 and 1997, Voss held a series of progressively senior positions within the Environmental Assessment and National Operations divisions. Personnel records indicate repeated involvement in emergency response activities during this period.

Voss was appointed Deputy Director of National Operations in 2003. She assumed the position of Director on 12 January 2009 following the retirement of Director Harold Mercer.

No interruption in employment with CAS has been recorded since her original appointment.
